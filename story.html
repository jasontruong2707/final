Our Story

From a sidewalk coffee cart to a cafe one block from campus.

Where we started

Common Table began in 2014 as a coffee cart on Jefferson Avenue, across the street from the main entrance to campus. Our founders had worked in restaurant kitchens for twelve years. At the time, there were few places near campus to get lunch, and most students either used the vending machines in their buildings or drove somewhere.

The cart sold coffee and muffins. Within the first semester, students asked for sandwiches, so the founders made them at home each night. By 2016 the line at noon was long, and they rented a small storefront one block away at 2700 Jefferson Avenue. We have stayed at that address since then.

Close to campus

The cafe is off campus, but the nearest classroom buildings are a five-minute walk away. Most of our customers are students and staff who come in between classes. Because the walk is short, catering orders for clubs and offices are carried over by our own staff.

How we cook

Most of the menu is made in our own kitchen. Soups are cooked each morning. Bread comes from a bakery twenty minutes away, and when it is in season, produce is bought from two farms in the county.

We keep prices low because most of our customers are students. A full lunch costs less than ten dollars.

Why "Common Table"

The first dining room had one long table because it was the only furniture that fit. People who did not know each other sat side by side. When the cafe was remodeled, we kept long shared tables on purpose.

Milestones

  1. 2014

    The coffee cart opens

    Coffee and muffins across from the campus entrance, weekdays from 9 AM to 1 PM.

  2. 2016

    A storefront of our own

    We rent a small room at 2700 Jefferson Avenue and add a sandwich counter.

  3. 2018

    Full kitchen

    A grill and a soup station are added, and hot lunch goes on the menu.

  4. 2021

    Catering for campus events

    Clubs and offices on campus can order sandwich trays and coffee for their meetings.

  5. 2023

    Remodel and salad bar

    We take over the empty shop next door. Seating doubles to 120, and a salad bar opens.

  6. 2025

    One million cups

    We pour our millionth cup of coffee, counted from the first day of the cart.
