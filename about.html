Common questions

Can I pay with my credit card?

Yes. Debit and credit cards, Apple Pay, and cash are accepted at our cafeteria.

Do you cater events or club meetings?

Yes. Trays of sandwiches, cookies, and coffee can be ordered three days ahead. Call or email us with the date and the number of people.

Do you have vegetarian and gluten-free food?

Items marked V or GF on the menu are vegetarian or gluten free. Tell the cashier about any allergy.

Can I order online?

Not on our website yet. You can order delivery from us on Uber Eats and DoorDash.
